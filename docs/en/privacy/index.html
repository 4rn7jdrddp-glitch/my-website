Personal data

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking technologies.

The contact form currently prepares a project brief on your own device. Your details are not transmitted to us or to any third party. If you choose to share the brief with us, we use your details only to respond to your inquiry.

Preferences stored in your browser

If you turn motion off, that preference is saved in your browser’s local storage so the website remembers it. It is never sent anywhere, and you can remove it by clearing your browser data.
